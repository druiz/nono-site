Nono

Your bots, on your iPhone.

Nono is an iPhone app for the AI bots that run on a computer you own: a Mac, a home server or a small cloud server. You chat with them, they put what needs you in a Feed of cards, and anything they do on your behalf waits for your yes.

Runs where you chooseYour bots live in nono-server, on your own machine. Nono talks to it directly.
You approveSending mail, changing a calendar, running a command: each asks on your phone first.
Your models, your plansA bot runs on Claude, ChatGPT or a local model, with your own account.

Google accounts

With Sign in with Google, your bots can read and search your Gmail and send the emails you approve, read your calendar and create events you approve, look up your contacts, and work with Google Drive files you pick or that a bot creates for you.

Your Google data travels from Google to your own nono-server, and from there to the AI model your bot uses, only to answer you. It never passes through a server run by Nono's developer. You can disconnect at any time from Nono or from your Google Account. Details are in the privacy policy.
